ENIAC–Magist Data Analysis

This repository contains a structured data analysis to evaluate whether Magist is a suitable strategic partner for ENIAC, a premium Apple-compatible accessories brand.
The analysis focuses on business-relevant criteria such as pricing structure, seller quality, delivery performance, and overall strategic fit with ENIAC’s premium positioning.

📌 Business Question

Does Magist meet ENIAC’s requirements as a potential partner platform, without compromising ENIAC’s premium brand standards?

📊 Analysis Scope

The analysis addresses the following key areas:
Product price levels (low, mid, premium)
Seller landscape and distribution
Delivery times and logistics performance
Strategic fit with a premium-focused portfolio
Risks related to brand dilution and customer expectations

📈 Key Findings 

Magist is strongly focused on low-price products
Premium-priced products are underrepresented
Delivery times and seller structure raise concerns for high customer expectations
Overall, Magist shows a limited strategic fit with ENIAC’s premium positioning
Detailed results and evidence are provided through SQL queries and Tableau visualizations in this repository.

🎯 Conclusion

Based on the analysis, Magist aligns better with low-cost, high-volume business models than with a premium brand strategy.
Any potential partnership would require careful consideration to avoid brand dilution and customer dissatisfaction.
